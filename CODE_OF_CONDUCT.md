# Code of Conduct

AgentDeck follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and make this a welcoming project for everyone,
whatever their background or experience. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't tolerated.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ucaboro). Reports are handled confidentially.
